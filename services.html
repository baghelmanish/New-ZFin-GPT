SERVICE 01

MCP Infrastructure for Legacy & Finance Software

Most older finance and business software wasn't built to work with AI. But if it has a REST API, the way most modern software talks to other software, we add an MCP translation layer on top of it. Think of MCP as giving your legacy system a way to "speak AI," allowing Claude & Zia to look things up, reconcile records, and take action inside it without needing a system rewrite.

Zero Code Rewrites
We don't need to rebuild your legacy software or your migration project. We connect directly to your existing REST API.
Role-Based Scoping
Specify exactly what an AI agent can read vs. act on, with 100% audit logging for full transparency.
💡 Real Enterprise Example
A finance department running a custom ERP with REST APIs gets an MCP layer. Now an AI agent can query vendor balances, verify invoice IDs, and output reconciled cash flow drafts in seconds.

How ZFin GPT MCP Deployment Works

  1. API Discovery: We review your financial REST APIs and map endpoints.
  2. MCP Layer Build: We stand up the lightweight MCP translation gateway.
  3. Permission Scoping: We configure read/write permissions per system.
  4. Agent Activation: Your team starts issuing queries to Claude or Zia (same system, instant AI capability).

SERVICE 02

Zoho Finance & Email Automation

Finance and client-facing teams spend hours every day processing incoming vendor invoices, payment confirmations, and client balance queries. We connect Zoho Books/Mail and Claude so those responses are automatically drafted and recorded, waiting for human approval.

Human Approval Safety Guard
Nothing sends or posts automatically without a human reviewer reviewing and clicking approve. You get automation speed with 100% human oversight.
Real Inbox Example
A vendor emails an invoice. Within seconds, a draft reply is waiting in your Zoho Mail drafts with invoice line items extracted into Zoho Books as a draft bill. You read it, check the total, and click approve.

Zoho + Claude Capabilities

  • Drafts prepared directly inside your existing Zoho Mail inbox
  • Automated invoice & receipt parsing into Zoho Books
  • Filters out noise, newsletters, and auto-responders automatically
  • Learns company tone, formal and threshold preferences
  • Flags missing information with a draft clarifying prompt
✓ 100% Zoho Native: Works inside Zoho Books, Zoho Mail, and Zoho CRM seamlessly.
